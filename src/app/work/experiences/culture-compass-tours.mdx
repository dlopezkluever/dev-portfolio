## Role Overview

As an independent software engineer and technical consultant, I led end-to-end technical execution across multiple projects for small organizations and local businesses. Projects ranged from lightweight marketing sites to production-grade booking, CRM, and automation systems.

## Key Achievements

### Independent Engineering & Consulting
- **End-to-End Delivery**: Led requirements gathering, system architecture, backend development, frontend implementation, deployment, and ongoing iteration across multiple independent projects
- **Full-Stack Applications**: Designed, built, and deployed full-stack web applications and internal tools ranging from marketing sites to production-grade booking, CRM, and automation systems
- **AI-First Workflows**: Applied AI-first development workflows to rapidly prototype, ship, and refine applications, leveraging GPT-based tools for code generation, customer communication, internal operations, and content creation

### Culture Compass Tours (Mexico City)
- **Full-Stack Website Development**: Engineered a comprehensive web application using Flask framework with PostgreSQL database and automated email response system serving 50+ customer inquiries monthly
- **Dynamic Booking System**: Implemented real-time booking capabilities with payment processing integration and automated confirmation workflows, increasing booking efficiency by 75%
- **SEO & Content Management**: Architected SEO-optimized content management system driving significant increases in organic search traffic
- **Brand Identity & Marketing**: Created comprehensive marketing assets including professional photography, fliers, business cards, banners, and digital collateral across 5+ channels
- **Business Strategy**: Authored detailed business plan and investor presentation materials, successfully securing initial funding and operational partnerships
- **Tour Operations**: Occasionally led tours on-site, gaining firsthand experience in customer service and cross-cultural business operations
